Life Groups

Grow in faith, knowledge, and relationships through small groups designed to help you belong.

Find Your Group

Filter by Day

Saturdays · 10:00 AM–12:00 PM

Life-Class Bible Study

Life-Class Bible Study is designed to help you explore Scripture deeply and understand how God's Word applies to your daily life. Through guided lessons, discussions, and reflection, participants grow in knowledge and faith. This group encourages questions, fosters accountability, and helps members walk out their beliefs with confidence.

What you'll experience: interactive Bible study discussions, practical application of God's Word, spiritual growth through accountability and reflection, and fellowship with like-minded believers.

Join Life-Class
3rd Tuesdays · 7:00–9:00 PM

Life-Line Night of Prayer

Life-Line Night of Prayer is a safe space to bring your burdens, lift others in prayer, and experience God's presence in community. Whether you're interceding for loved ones, the church, or the world, prayer becomes a shared journey that strengthens faith and relationships.

What you'll experience: powerful corporate prayer and intercession, support and encouragement from fellow members, opportunities to pray for personal and community needs, and deepened intimacy with God.

Join Life-Line Prayer
4th Wednesdays · 7:00–9:00 PM

Life-Pathways Empowerment

Life-Pathways Empowerment equips members to step into purpose and potential. Through mentorship, workshops, and life-guidance sessions, participants discover their unique gifts, gain practical life tools, and receive encouragement to grow spiritually, emotionally, and professionally.

What you'll experience: mentorship and personal development, life skills and spiritual guidance, goal setting and accountability, and empowerment to live purposefully.

Join Life-Pathways
