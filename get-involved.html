Ministries at One Life Church

God never designed us to only attend. He designed us to belong, to serve, and to sow.

Our Ministries

Pastoral Ministry

Our pastoral team provides guidance, spiritual care, and leadership support to the One Life family. They help you grow in faith, understand God's Word, and walk confidently in your purpose — mentoring, counseling, and equipping you for meaningful life and service.

Learn More →

Breakfast Café @1352

Start your day with fellowship and nourishment. Breakfast Café @1352 is more than just a meal — it's a place to connect, share, and care for one another in a welcoming environment.

Join Breakfast Café →

Life-Class Bible Study

Grow in faith, knowledge, and relationships through small groups, Life-Class Bible studies, and Life-Pathways Empowerment programs. Everyone has a place to belong and grow.

Join a Small Group →

Youth & Children's Ministry

Our youth and children's programs equip the next generation with faith, life skills, and community — preparing young people to live boldly and serve faithfully. Every child has a safe, welcoming place to learn and grow.

Learn More →

Outreach / Sponsorship Lunch Program

Bring hope and practical support to local schools and communities. Our sponsorship lunch program and outreach initiatives provide meals, resources, and encouragement to those in need.
