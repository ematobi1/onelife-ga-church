Pastoral Ministry

Guidance, Spiritual Care, and Support

Our pastoral team provides guidance, spiritual care, and leadership support to the One Life family. They help you grow in faith, understand God's Word, and walk confidently in your purpose — here to mentor, counsel, and support you.

If you're navigating grief, a difficult relationship, a crisis of faith, or just need someone to talk to, reach out. This is a judgment-free space.
