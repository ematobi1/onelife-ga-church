    Walking in Faith

    Welcome to One Life Church

    We are a people-focused, family-oriented church in Lawrenceville, Georgia where lives are refreshed, hope is restored, and purpose is awakened.

    No matter where life has taken you, or what season you are walking through, we want you to know — you are seen, you are loved, and you belong here.

    At One Life Church, we believe God is still writing your story. We exist to refresh people in their purpose, renew them in His promises, and release the potential He has already placed within them. This is not just a church you attend — this is a family you grow with.

    Senior Pastor, One Life Church

    Meet Pastor Kennard M. Weever

    Pastor Kennard M. Weever is a shepherd with a heart for people and a passion for purpose. His calling is rooted in seeing lives refreshed, faith strengthened, and potential awakened through the love and truth of God.

    With compassion, humility, and vision, Pastor Weever leads One Life Church with a commitment to reach people where they are and walk with them toward who God has called them to be. He believes church is not just a place you attend — it is a family you grow with.
